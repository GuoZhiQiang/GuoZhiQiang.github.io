FAQ

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open any locked skill or the Me tab, and tap “Restore Purchases”. Apple keeps the purchase record, so no separate account is needed.

What's free?

18 of the 22 skills, including CPR, choking rescue and stopping bleeding. The other advanced skills can be unlocked one at a time by watching a short ad, or all at once with Pro.

What does Pro include?

Every skill, including ones added later, and no ads. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress and favorites are stored only on your device. Deleting the app erases them, and they don't move to a new phone automatically.

Daily reminders don't arrive?

Check Settings › Notifications › Time Traveler and make sure notifications are allowed.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In regions where it applies (such as the EEA and UK), tap “Privacy Settings” on the Me tab or on any locked skill. Tracking permission can be changed any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Is this safe?

The skills are for education and history. Treat anything involving fire, chemicals or medicine with care, and call your local emergency number in a real emergency.
