Summary

Time Traveler's Handbook (“the app”) is made by an independent developer. The app has no accounts and the developer does not collect, receive or sell your personal information. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below. Buying Pro turns ads off completely.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer:

  • learning progress, mastered skills and favorites;
  • daily reminder settings;
  • skills unlocked by watching ads, and a cached copy of your purchases so unlocks work offline.

Home Screen widgets read the same on-device data through an App Group. Deleting the app erases all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you haven't bought Pro, the app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • performance and crash diagnostics of the ad SDK;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads still appear but are less personalized, and the IDFA is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice later via “Privacy Settings” on the Me tab or on any locked skill.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment and account details; the developer never receives your name, Apple Account or payment information. The app only receives Apple's proof of which items you own, to unlock content. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved, and you can turn them off any time in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
