LoopTune Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Your videos and recordings stay on your device

LoopTune processes videos, audio files and voice recordings entirely on your iPhone. We have no servers and never upload, view or store your media. Videos are chosen with Apple's system Photos picker, so the app cannot see the rest of your library. Photo library access is "add only" and is used to save the videos you export. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and recordings are saved in the app on your device until you delete them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt), coarse location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can decline tracking at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change their choice in LoopTune › Settings › Ad privacy choices. Buying Pro removes all ads, and the ads SDK then makes no ad requests.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

LoopTune is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
