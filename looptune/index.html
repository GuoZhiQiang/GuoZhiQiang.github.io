Frequently asked questions

How do I add music to a video?

Tap Choose from Photos or Choose from Files, then Add background audio. Pick a track, adjust the volumes and tap Export › Save to Photos.

The music is shorter than my video.

Keep Loop background audio on and the music repeats until the video ends. If the music is longer, it's trimmed to fit and fades out.

Can I use a Voice Memos recording?

Yes. In Voice Memos tap the recording, then Share › Save to Files. In LoopTune go to Mine › Import from Files. You can also record directly with Mine › Record now.

Can I use the built-in music commercially?

Yes. All built-in music and ambient sounds were created by us and are royalty-free for personal and commercial videos, including social media and YouTube. You may not resell or redistribute the audio files on their own.

Export failed or the video won't save.

Allow LoopTune to add photos in Settings › LoopTune › Photos. Make sure your iPhone has enough free storage — exports need roughly twice the size of the original video.

I bought Pro on another device.

Open Settings › Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account.
